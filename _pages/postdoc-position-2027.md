## Position Overview

Doshisha University invites applications for one postdoctoral researcher to participate in a joint research project with Professor Yury Korolev at the University of Bath, UK. The position is funded through the Royal Society project “ISPF International Collaboration Awards 2025 Round 2 – Japan: **Operator Learning for Gradient Flows with Applications in Imaging**.”

The project focuses primarily on the mathematical foundations of operator learning. Potential research topics include:

- Operator learning
- Gradient flows
- Convex analysis
- Functional analysis
- Inverse problems
- Neural operators
- Transformers

Applicants are not expected to have expertise in all of these areas. However, they should have a strong interest in these topics and be motivated to investigate related research problems. The successful candidate will be expected to collaborate with the project members and contribute actively to the preparation and submission of research papers to international journals.

International applications are strongly encouraged. Research activities and academic communication can be conducted in English, and proficiency in Japanese is not required.

## Qualifications

Applicants must meet the following requirements:

- Hold a PhD in mathematics or a closely related discipline, or expect to obtain one by the starting date.
- Be able to conduct research and communicate academically in English.
- Japanese-language proficiency is not required.
